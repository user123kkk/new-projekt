# Contrast effect
Kind: psychological mechanism

## Definition
"Your brain can't judge value in isolation." Includes price anchoring: "salespeople anchor prices high before showing you the real offer."

Note: Anchor must be a real price (najsh). The wine example is alcohol and not reusable.
