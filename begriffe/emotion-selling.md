# Emotion selling
Kind: psychological mechanism

## Definition
"You don't sell the product, you sell emotions." Merged synonyms: sell the feeling first (12), emotional content (25), people buy the product they like best (33).

Note: Conditional wherever emotions are produced by untrue content.

## Used in
- [[02-sell-the-after-not-the-what]] – "They buy the confidence boost and compliments they will be getting."
- [[11-you-sell-emotions-not-the-product]] – "You don't sell the product, you sell emotions."
- [[12-emotions-are-caught-sell-the-feeling-first]] – "sell the feeling first and the product second"
- [[25-emotional-content-is-remembered]] – "If people don't feel something, they won't remember anything."
- [[33-people-buy-the-product-they-like-best]] – "Make people like you best and that's when your product automatically becomes the best product for them."
