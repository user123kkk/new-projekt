# Emotion selling
Kind: psychological mechanism

## Definition
"You don't sell the product, you sell emotions." Merged synonyms: sell the feeling first (12), emotional content (25), people buy the product they like best (33).

Note: Conditional wherever emotions are produced by untrue content.
