# Bundle pricing
Kind: tactic

## Definition
"Instead of selling one bag and one watch for $49, say buy a bag for $49 and get a new watch for free. The second option feels like a better deal." (38)

Note: Not usable as shown: the same total price with the main item relabelled and the second item called 'free' is a false 'free' and an artificial anchor (rules 1, 4). A bundle is allowed when it is really cheaper than the single prices and the single prices are shown.
