# Reciprocity
Kind: psychological mechanism

## Definition
"People are more likely to buy when they receive value first." (44). Give a free trial, free sample or free resource before selling.

Note: Conditional. The gift must really be free, trial terms stated (rule 3), no hidden obligation or data collection (rules 6, 7).
