# So you can method
Kind: tactic

## Definition
"You take your product or feature, add So You Can, and then finish with the outcome." (48). Examples: "a home alarm system so you can feel safe at night"; "noise-canceling headphones so you can get your work done with no distractions".

Note: The outcome named must be what the product really delivers (rules 1, 5). Same direction as desired-outcome, but a concrete sentence formula.
