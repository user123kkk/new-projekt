# Cost of inaction
Kind: tactic

## Definition
Show the customer what they lose by not acting; "To each goal, there is also an anti-goal." Merged synonyms: anti-goal (11), pain of standing still (21).

Note: Rated conditional in 11 and 21: only real losses may be shown.
