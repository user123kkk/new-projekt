# Social proof
Kind: tactic

## Definition
"testimonials, case studies, and reviews that reinforce positive experiences others have had with your brand"; data, statistics, studies (36).

Note: Fabricated social proof is excluded (GRUNDSATZ: reviews, user numbers).
