# Brand perception
Kind: brand concept

## Definition
"your brand is about what people think and feel about you" (03); "The only P branding is about is perception." (20). Merged synonyms: perception, brand feeling, overall presence (31).

Note: Rated conditional where 'perception over reality' is read as permission to misrepresent.
