# Improve an existing offer
Kind: tactic

## Definition
"You can take something that already works and make it just 5% better." (51) Method: pick a product people already buy, read what customers complain about (price, delivery time, customer experience), solve at least one complaint better than the competition.

Note: Existing spending is demand for the incumbent, not proof of demand for the new offer. The video itself says "being slightly better doesn't guarantee success"; the offer still has to be found and trusted.
