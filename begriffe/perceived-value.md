# Perceived value
Kind: psychological mechanism

## Definition
"the perceived value of experiencing this emotion has to be greater than the perceived costs to experience the emotion" (11); price objection is "about the value they perceive from it" (22). Synonym: perceived quality (28).

Note: Costs and terms must be disclosed so perceived costs are accurate (gharar).
