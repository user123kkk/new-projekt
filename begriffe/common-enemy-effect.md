# Common enemy effect
Kind: psychological mechanism

## Definition
"when we share a common enemy with someone, we feel a stronger bond with them." (43). Applied to brands: the brand names what its customers dislike and stands against it.

Note: Conditional. The "enemy" may only be a problem, a condition or an idea, never a person or a group (order of authority 1); no invented threat (rule 1); stated openly (rules 6, 7).
