# Pain of paying
Kind: psychological mechanism

## Definition
The act of committing to buy is felt as a small pain; "The pain of paying actually lights up the same brain regions as physical pain."

Note: Distinct from pain-of-the-problem: this is the pain of the payment itself, not the pain of the unsolved problem. Claim, unverified in the source (no study named).
