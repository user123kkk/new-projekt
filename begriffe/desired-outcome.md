# Desired outcome
Kind: psychological mechanism

## Definition
"They want the better version of themselves." The customer buys the outcome or the 'after', not the product. Merged synonyms: desired transformation (02), goal (11), outcome (33).

Note: Station 29 (instant benefit) is kept under immediate-reward-bias because it opposes 02 (long-term transformation).
