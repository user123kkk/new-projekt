# Negative first ordering
Kind: tactic

## Definition
"Start with the negative and end with the positive, because people remember the last thing you say most." (41) Examples: "our product is really expensive, but it is the best in the world"; "if you don't see the results you want, you'll get your money back".

Note: The negative must be a real, fully stated fact (price, conditions); the positive must be true (rules 1, 3, 5, 7). Contradicted in emphasis by station 30 (the beginning matters more than the end).
