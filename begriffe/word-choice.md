# Word choice
Kind: tactic

## Definition
Replacing single words or phrases in marketing text with others that sound better: "Sign up" to "Start now", "Best" to "Top rated", "Only here" to "Exclusive", "Sale" to "Special offer", "Must have" to "Essential" (49).

Note: A swap changes the sound, not the claim. The claim behind the new word must be true (rules 1, 4, 5); a swap that only softens an untrue claim is deception.
