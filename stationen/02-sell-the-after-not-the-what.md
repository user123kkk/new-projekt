## Assessment
1. **usable** – Framing only.
2. **usable** – Generalisation; claim, unverified.
3. **usable** – Claim, unverified.
4. **usable** – Claim, unverified.
5. **conditional** – Usable if the offer actually supports the stated future. Required change: no promise of a result the product does not deliver (ghish).
6. **conditional** – Usable if confidence is described as an experience, not guaranteed. Required change: no promise of compliments or social outcomes as certain. Product category is not itself a checkpoint hit.
7. **conditional** – Required change: the future shown must be realistic and attainable with the product (ghish, gharar).
8. **conditional** – Claim, unverified. Same condition as statement 7.
9. **conditional** – Same condition as statement 7: the 'after' must be real.
