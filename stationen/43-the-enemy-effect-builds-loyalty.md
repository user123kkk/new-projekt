## Assessment
1. **conditional** – "Psychology says" has no named source and "the reason" is a single-cause absolute: claim, unverified. Required change: the "enemy" must never be a person or a group of people (order of authority 1: enmity and hatred of people is no basis for a brand); it may only be a problem or a condition. Rule 7 (mechanism allowed when true and nothing concealed) and rule 1 (the named problem must be real).
2. **usable** – Framing.
3. **usable** – Rhetorical question; the setting is only a setting.
4. **conditional** – Describes people bonding by shared hatred of a person: that is backbiting and enmity (order of authority 1). Required change: do not reuse this scene as the example; show the bond over a shared problem instead (e.g. both are annoyed by the same wasted time). Rule 7.
5. **conditional** – Claim, unverified. Same change as 4: usable only for a bond that does not rest on hatred of a person.
6. **usable** – Names the mechanism; whether "enemy effect" is an established term is unverified.
7. **conditional** – Claim, unverified. The mechanism (shared opposition creates closeness) is usable when the shared "enemy" is a problem, an idea or a condition, never a person, group or the customers of a competitor. Rule 7: nothing concealed; the brand states openly what it stands against.
8. **conditional** – "Every cult brand" is an unproven absolute. "Trick" and "cult" frame loyalty as something produced without the customer noticing and as blind devotion. Required change: say openly what the brand is against (rule 6: anything that would have to be concealed falls under ghish; rule 7); aim for loyalty from real value, not for devotion. Claim, unverified.
9. **usable** – Example about a named brand; claim, unverified. The "enemy" is a state (laziness), which is the permitted form of an enemy.
10. **usable** – Example about a named brand; claim, unverified; transcript spelling "Red Bulls" kept.
11. **usable** – Example about a named brand; claim, unverified; transcript spelling "Teslas" kept.
12. **conditional** – Finding out what the customer dislikes or struggles with is usable (customer research). Required change: "hates" is read as the real problem or frustration, never hatred of a person; "must" and "cult brand" are unproven; the goal is loyal customers, not a cult. Claim, unverified.
13. **conditional** – Required change: the "enemy" is a real problem the customer has, named truthfully and in its real size, with no invented threat and no pressure through fear (rule 1, rule 7; compare station 37 statement 4); never a person, a group or a competitor's people.
14. **conditional** – Claim, unverified: loyalty through a shared enemy. Same conditions as 12 and 13. Sentence is incomplete as transcribed.
15. **not usable** – A figure shown as a measured result with no source, no sample and no context. Rule 1 (no false claims about user numbers or reviews) and rule 5 (proof for strong claims). Usable only with real, measured data from our own users and the source shown.
