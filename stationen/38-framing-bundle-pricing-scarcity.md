## Assessment
1. **conditional** – 'Anything to anyone' is an absolute promise (rule 5, exaggeration) and ignores the limit of the product. Required change: only halal products (order of authority 1, rule 15: selling to everyone is allowed as long as the product is halal); no promise that a technique sells anything.
2. **usable** – Names a technique. The use is judged in statements 3-5.
3. **usable** – The two wordings state the same fact (20% fat = 80% fat free), so both are true. Claim about the equivalence is arithmetic. Usable as long as both frames state the same true fact.
4. **usable** – Claim, unverified: no study named. Consistent with rule 7 only while both wordings are true.
5. **conditional** – Claim, unverified. Required change: framing may choose the positive wording only when the fact is identical and nothing is hidden (rule 7, rule 1 deception, rule 3 gharar); no frame that leaves out a cost, a limit or a risk.
6. **usable** – Names a technique. The use is judged in statements 7-9.
7. **usable** – Describes selling two items together for one price; no checkpoint touched. Note: audio $49, screen $49.99.
8. **not usable** – As shown, the offer is the same price (screen: $49.99 = $49.99), the bag is relabelled from $30 to $49.99 and the watch is called "free" although it is paid inside that price. 'Free' for something included in the price is a false claim (rule 1, ghish) and an artificial price anchor (rule 4, najsh); the real price of the bag is hidden (rule 3). A permissible bundle says: bag and watch together for the real bundle price, with the real single prices shown, if the bundle is really cheaper. Only a watch that truly costs nothing extra may be called free.
9. **conditional** – Claim, unverified. Usable only if the second option is a better deal in fact. Required change: the saving must be real and stated with the real single prices (rule 4, rule 1). Making an identical offer 'feel' better by relabelling is not allowed (rule 7: not against the customer's interest; rule 1).
10. **conditional** – Names a technique. Required change: scarcity may be used only when real (rule 1, rule 4); see statements 12-14.
11. **usable** – Claim, unverified.
12. **conditional** – Required change: 'only a few left' only when it is true and current; the number shown ("only 5") must be the real stock, no counters that are invented, reset or permanent (rule 1, rule 4, rule 6: nothing concealed). No countdown or stock text as a pressure tool without a basis.
13. **conditional** – Claim, unverified. Same condition as statement 12: the eagerness may only follow from real scarcity (rule 1, rule 7).
14. **conditional** – Claim, unverified. Fear of missing out is acceptable only about real, verifiable limits (rule 1, rule 4, rule 7: not to move a person against their own interest). Not allowed for decisions that need time or a clear trial and price (rule 3, gharar). See [[fear-of-missing-out]].
