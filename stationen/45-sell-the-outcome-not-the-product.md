## Assessment
1. **usable** – Rhetorical device (product picture against outcome picture).
2. **usable** – Claim, unverified; "don't care" is an absolute (compare 33 statement 3, which says people care about the product once they understand the outcome).
3. **usable** – Example with a permissible product; claim, unverified.
4. **usable** – Claim, unverified.
5. **conditional** – "You'll never have to sell the product again" is an absolute promise of an effect (rule 5). Required change: no promise that outcome positioning ends the need to sell; and the outcome sold must be one the product really delivers (rule 1, ghish). Compare 37 statement 7 ("sells itself").
