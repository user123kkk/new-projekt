## Assessment
1. **conditional** – "Anything to anyone" is an absolute over-promise (rule 5). Required change: only a halal product (rule 15: selling to everyone is allowed as long as the product is halal), and no claim that every product can be sold to every person.
2. **usable** – Names the tactic.
3. **usable** – Claim, unverified.
4. **conditional** – Required change: only real reviews from real buyers; no bought, invented or selected-to-mislead reviews (rule 1). Claim, unverified.
5. **usable** – Names the mechanism (reciprocity). Rule 7: allowed when true and nothing concealed.
6. **conditional** – Giving something free is usable. Required changes: a free trial states duration, price afterwards and how to cancel (rule 3, gharar); "free" must really be free, with no hidden obligation or hidden data collection (rule 6, rule 1).
7. **usable** – Claim, unverified. Rule 7: the gift is real and has no hidden strings.
8. **usable** – Claim, unverified; "everything" is an absolute (compare 04, 31, 33).
9. **not usable** – Making buyers pay five times more for the same thing is deception and an artificial price anchor: rule 1, rule 4 (najsh), rule 5. Same ruling as station 28 statement 11. The usable core is limited to: a real, honestly named difference (design, service, experience) may carry a higher price; "the same thing" at 5x may not.
10. **not usable** – A result figure with no source, period or context. Rule 1 (no false claims about prices, user numbers or sales) and rule 5 (proof). Usable only as our own real figure with the source shown.
