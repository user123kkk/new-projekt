3. **usable** – Claim, unverified.
4. **conditional** – Required change: the belief must rest on true information and the thing must be worth what is paid (rules 1, 5, ghish); a belief produced by exaggeration or concealment is deception. The product must be permissible (rule 15; order of authority 1).
5. **usable** – Claim, unverified; hyperbole ("completely").
6. **usable** – Claim, unverified ("Every"). Matches [[14-sell-water-to-the-thirsty]]: customers pay where there is a reason.
7. **usable** – Claim, unverified.
8. **conditional** – Convenience, time saved, less uncertainty and a solved problem are usable as reasons. Required change: "would otherwise cost much more" is a claim about the customer's avoided cost; it must be true and demonstrable (rules 1, 5).
9. **usable** – Claim, unverified. It does not license a price unrelated to real value; see [[22-price-is-never-the-problem]].
10. **conditional** – Usable as a principle. Required change: the outcome must really be delivered; no outcome promised that cannot be shown (rules 1, 5), and the price must not exceed the real value (see 22, statements 6 and 7).
11. **conditional** – Required change: understanding human behaviour is used to serve the customer and describe the product truthfully, not to move a person against their own interest (rule 7).
12. **conditional** – Wants, frustrations and willingness to pay are usable as market research (compare K1 in IDEEN.md). Required change: do not exploit acute distress; a religious obligation is not treated as a market gap (rule 11); the product must be permissible (rule 15).
13. **usable** – Claim, unverified. Opinion about motive.
14. **usable** – Claim, unverified; metaphor ("money moves").
15. **conditional** – Required change: "a reason to move toward you" must be a true reason, meaning real value; no invented reasons, price anchors or scarcity (rules 1, 4).
