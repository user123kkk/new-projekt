# P5 Deutsch lesen: web research (graded A1-A2 German texts, read aloud, tap-to-translate)

Date of research: 2026-10-28. Tools: WebSearch + WebFetch only. About 45 searches plus about 45 fetches. Complete.

Method notes (read first):
- `(fetch)` = returned by WebFetch's summarising model, not re-checked against raw HTML; high, not proven. The fetcher caps quotes at about 125 characters, so many reviews are opening fragments only.
- `[search summary, unverified]` = taken only from a WebSearch result summary, page not read.
- No usernames. Apple App Store dates are exact.
- Gaps (honest): reddit.com is blocked for WebFetch and web search returned no reddit threads, so r/German and r/learngerman quotes are missing; Google Play not reachable; goethe.de, dw.com, learngerman.dw.com, TikTok and Instagram profiles returned 403/blocked; Duolingo, LingQ, Babbel and Readle review feeds returned empty. Section 3 is therefore App Store, Hacker News, forum and test.de only.

## 6. GRUNDSATZ check (no music, no images of people)

- Format needs only text, voice and shapes: Readle's own claim is "Native audio for every sentence" and "Tap any word" (fetch); nothing in the core job requires music or people images. A faceless short video can show German text on screen with a TTS or recorded voice and a translation line.
- Conflicting competitor practice found:
  - Music: Beelinguapp offers "unlimited music" content, and one review asks for the opposite ("Ruins all audio quality ... when I want some background noise when reading", i.e. audio mixing issues) (section 3). Language-learning TikToks use memes and film clips (Learn German Camel: "picks up memes in German, as well as bits from German films and TV") (fetch).
  - People/avatars: most TikTok accounts in the Lingoda list are presenter-led (face on screen) and Learn German for Life, Deutsch Eins use a presenter with subtitles (fetch).
  - Images: Beelinguapp story covers are AI images ("Covers are AI slop"); Readle and Eppika use cover art.

## 7. Summary

1. Demand pool is real: about 300k+ people start an integration course per year in DE (falling: 363k to 307k), about 67k places in AT, 15 m learners worldwide; only about 55 % reach B1, so many are stuck at A1-A2.
2. The exact job (graded A1-A2 text, native audio, tap-translate) is already done by Readle (DE company, 1,787 DE ratings, 4.7, A1-C1, 23 languages incl. Arabic, 9,99 EUR/month or 69,99/year). Eppika, Magellanister, Story Owl, DeuTale, Beelinguapp and LingQ are the same job at 2-10 EUR/month.
3. Free offers cover part of it: nachrichtenleicht (audio, simple German, no translation, German only), DW (A1-B1 video course; reading content B2+), VHS-Lernportal (A1-B1, menus in 18-19 languages, free), BAMF "Ankommen" and Goethe "Stadt der Wörter" (the two Warentest-recommended apps, free).
4. Strongest gap (inferred from sources, not proven): texts that are truly A1, short, everyday and not news or classics, with translation into Arabic/Turkish/Ukrainian/Farsi. Reviews say "not for the very beginner" and that competitors move to news and AI text. No complaint says "no Arabic/Ukrainian translation"; that wish is not evidenced.
5. Realistic price: 3-6 EUR/month or 20-40 EUR/year sits below Readle and the willingness quotes (10-15 USD/month, only from English-speaking HN readers). Integration course learners pay 2.29 EUR/hour course fee or are exempt, so budget is low; the free VHS/BAMF/DW tools set the ceiling.
6. Main risk: Readle and cheap clones exist and AI-generated clones are multiplying; free state tools are good; short-video evidence for this niche is thin (no TikTok numbers; only YouTube sizes, with Easy German at 3.35 m subscribers on people-led formats).
7. GRUNDSATZ: doable with text + voice + shapes; the quality of the voice and the lack of AI cover art/music are differentiators users complain about elsewhere. Conflicts: presenter-led and music/meme-based short-video norms.
8. Not verified: reddit sentiment, Google Play, TikTok sizes, B2B prices. Do not treat this as proof of demand; it shows competition and a sample of dissatisfaction.
9. Verdict for P5: market exists but the one-job slot is occupied by Readle; room only with an A1-only, ad-free, low-price angle and proof from a demand test.
