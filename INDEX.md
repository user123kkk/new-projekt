## Clusters
### Brand is perception
Stations: [[03-your-brand-is-the-feeling-not-the-logo]], [[07-marketing-and-branding-are-yin-and-yang]], [[09-branding-is-for-any-company]], [[18-halo-effect]], [[20-branding-is-the-1p-model-perception]]

Reason: All define branding as what people think and feel (impression, perception), not as logo or product; 07 adds the marketing/branding split, 18 the halo mechanism.

### Product is not decisive
Stations: [[04-there-is-no-winning-product]], [[31-best-product-with-bad-branding-loses]], [[33-people-buy-the-product-they-like-best]], [[28-same-wine-different-price-experience]]

Reason: All claim the better product does not win; branding, liking or presentation decides. 28 is the worked example (same wine, two prices).

### Emotion as the driver
Stations: [[02-sell-the-after-not-the-what]], [[11-you-sell-emotions-not-the-product]], [[12-emotions-are-caught-sell-the-feeling-first]], [[25-emotional-content-is-remembered]], [[33-people-buy-the-product-they-like-best]]

Reason: All state that people buy or remember emotions and outcomes rather than features.

### Loss, pain and cost of not acting
Stations: [[01-reduce-the-pain-of-paying]], [[21-people-want-to-stop-losing]], [[11-you-sell-emotions-not-the-product]], [[37-sell-the-problem-first]], [[14-sell-water-to-the-thirsty]]

Reason: All use pain or loss as the motive: pain of paying (01), loss aversion (21), anti-goal (11), pain of the problem (37, 14).

### Problem first, then relief
Stations: [[14-sell-water-to-the-thirsty]], [[17-a-problem-well-explained-is-a-solution-half-sold]], [[37-sell-the-problem-first]], [[21-people-want-to-stop-losing]]

Reason: All start from the customer's problem (already painful, well explained, felt) and present the offer as relief.

### Clarity and simplicity
Stations: [[10-the-five-year-old-rule]], [[15-three-questions-every-customer-asks]], [[24-attention-comes-from-clarity-not-frequency]], [[27-the-more-people-think-the-less-they-trust]], [[34-one-message-one-offer-one-action]], [[13-animations-that-turn-your-message-into-a-story]], [[30-content-fails-in-the-first-seconds]], [[17-a-problem-well-explained-is-a-solution-half-sold]]

Reason: All claim that unclear or complex messages reduce attention, trust and conversion.

### Curiosity and complexity (counter-cluster to clarity)
Stations: [[08-open-a-loop-zagarnik-effect]], [[16-illusion-of-innovation]]

Reason: Both recommend gaps or complexity to create curiosity; they contradict the clarity cluster.

### Price and perceived value
Stations: [[22-price-is-never-the-problem]], [[26-use-the-power-of-contrast]], [[28-same-wine-different-price-experience]], [[11-you-sell-emotions-not-the-product]], [[01-reduce-the-pain-of-paying]]

Reason: All treat price as a function of perceived value, reference points or cost felt (22, 26, 28, 11, 01).

### Understanding the customer
Stations: [[17-a-problem-well-explained-is-a-solution-half-sold]], [[19-sell-to-everyone-sell-to-no-one]], [[23-the-customer-is-the-hero-of-the-story]], [[36-confirmation-bias-say-what-people-already-think]], [[15-three-questions-every-customer-asks]], [[02-sell-the-after-not-the-what]]

Reason: All make the customer's situation, beliefs or story the starting point of the message.

### Covert influence and manipulation wording
Stations: [[06-priming-makes-people-buy-without-a-word]], [[12-emotions-are-caught-sell-the-feeling-first]], [[26-use-the-power-of-contrast]], [[35-make-them-feel-they-found-it]], [[36-confirmation-bias-say-what-people-already-think]], [[37-sell-the-problem-first]], [[29-sell-the-instant-benefit]], [[16-illusion-of-innovation]]

Reason: All contain techniques or wording that work without the customer's awareness, or explicitly name manipulation; the main touchpoint with the deception checkpoint.

### Content, attention and comment/DM calls to action
Stations: [[24-attention-comes-from-clarity-not-frequency]], [[25-emotional-content-is-remembered]], [[27-the-more-people-think-the-less-they-trust]], [[30-content-fails-in-the-first-seconds]], [[13-animations-that-turn-your-message-into-a-story]], [[10-the-five-year-old-rule]], [[05-study-what-works-particl]]

### Promotions and service offers
Stations: [[05-study-what-works-particl]], [[10-the-five-year-old-rule]], [[13-animations-that-turn-your-message-into-a-story]]

Reason: Each promotes a third-party tool (05) or the creator's own service (10, 13) with a DM call to action.

### Funnel diagnosis
Stations: [[32-conversion-problem-or-traffic-problem]], [[01-reduce-the-pain-of-paying]], [[14-sell-water-to-the-thirsty]], [[24-attention-comes-from-clarity-not-frequency]]

Reason: 32 splits problems into traffic and conversion; 01 (checkout) and 14, 24 (finding and getting attention) sit on either side.
