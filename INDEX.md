## Clusters
### Brand is perception
Stations: [[03-your-brand-is-the-feeling-not-the-logo]], [[07-marketing-and-branding-are-yin-and-yang]], [[09-branding-is-for-any-company]], [[18-halo-effect]], [[20-branding-is-the-1p-model-perception]], [[44-social-proof-reciprocity-branding]], [[46-selling-versus-storytelling]]

Reason: All define branding as what people think and feel (impression, perception), not as logo or product; 07 adds the marketing/branding split, 18 the halo mechanism. 44 (branding is everything) and 46 (storytelling turns products into symbols) add the brand-as-meaning side.

### Product is not decisive
Stations: [[04-there-is-no-winning-product]], [[31-best-product-with-bad-branding-loses]], [[33-people-buy-the-product-they-like-best]], [[28-same-wine-different-price-experience]], [[45-sell-the-outcome-not-the-product]], [[44-social-proof-reciprocity-branding]]

Reason: All claim the better product does not win; branding, liking or presentation decides. 28 is the worked example (same wine, two prices). 45 says customers care about the outcome, 44 that branding outweighs the product (its "five times more for the same thing" part is not usable).

### Emotion as the driver
Stations: [[02-sell-the-after-not-the-what]], [[11-you-sell-emotions-not-the-product]], [[12-emotions-are-caught-sell-the-feeling-first]], [[25-emotional-content-is-remembered]], [[33-people-buy-the-product-they-like-best]], [[42-sell-the-human-desire-behind-the-product]], [[45-sell-the-outcome-not-the-product]], [[46-selling-versus-storytelling]], [[48-the-so-you-can-method]]

Reason: All state that people buy or remember emotions and outcomes rather than features. 42, 45, 46 and 48 add desire, outcome, meaning and "so you can" as the thing sold.

### Loss, pain and cost of not acting
Stations: [[01-reduce-the-pain-of-paying]], [[21-people-want-to-stop-losing]], [[11-you-sell-emotions-not-the-product]], [[37-sell-the-problem-first]], [[14-sell-water-to-the-thirsty]], [[38-framing-bundle-pricing-scarcity]], [[43-the-enemy-effect-builds-loyalty]]

Reason: All use pain or loss as the motive: pain of paying (01), loss aversion (21), anti-goal (11), pain of the problem (37, 14). 38 uses fear of missing out (scarcity), 43 uses a shared problem ("enemy") as the lever.

### Problem first, then relief
Stations: [[14-sell-water-to-the-thirsty]], [[17-a-problem-well-explained-is-a-solution-half-sold]], [[37-sell-the-problem-first]], [[21-people-want-to-stop-losing]], [[43-the-enemy-effect-builds-loyalty]], [[40-four-buying-stages-schwartz]]

Reason: All start from the customer's problem (already painful, well explained, felt) and present the offer as relief. 43 starts from what the customer hates (a problem, never a person), 40 from the stage "problem aware".

### Clarity and simplicity
Stations: [[10-the-five-year-old-rule]], [[15-three-questions-every-customer-asks]], [[24-attention-comes-from-clarity-not-frequency]], [[27-the-more-people-think-the-less-they-trust]], [[34-one-message-one-offer-one-action]], [[13-animations-that-turn-your-message-into-a-story]], [[30-content-fails-in-the-first-seconds]], [[17-a-problem-well-explained-is-a-solution-half-sold]], [[39-people-scan-they-dont-read]], [[47-people-dont-read-they-scan]], [[49-five-words-to-swap-in-marketing]]

Reason: All claim that unclear or complex messages reduce attention, trust and conversion. 39 (scannable; 47 is its duplicate) and 49 (plain word swaps) add low reading effort.

### Curiosity and complexity (counter-cluster to clarity)
Stations: [[08-open-a-loop-zagarnik-effect]], [[16-illusion-of-innovation]]

Reason: Both recommend gaps or complexity to create curiosity; they contradict the clarity cluster.

### Price and perceived value
Stations: [[22-price-is-never-the-problem]], [[26-use-the-power-of-contrast]], [[28-same-wine-different-price-experience]], [[11-you-sell-emotions-not-the-product]], [[01-reduce-the-pain-of-paying]], [[38-framing-bundle-pricing-scarcity]], [[44-social-proof-reciprocity-branding]], [[50-understand-why-money-moves]]

Reason: All treat price as a function of perceived value, reference points or cost felt (22, 26, 28, 11, 01). 38 (bundle framing), 44 ("five times more") and 50 (value decided by the outcome) add how a price is presented and what decides value.

### Understanding the customer
Stations: [[17-a-problem-well-explained-is-a-solution-half-sold]], [[19-sell-to-everyone-sell-to-no-one]], [[23-the-customer-is-the-hero-of-the-story]], [[36-confirmation-bias-say-what-people-already-think]], [[15-three-questions-every-customer-asks]], [[02-sell-the-after-not-the-what]], [[40-four-buying-stages-schwartz]], [[43-the-enemy-effect-builds-loyalty]], [[50-understand-why-money-moves]], [[51-you-dont-need-to-invent-to-become-rich]]

Reason: All make the customer's situation, beliefs or story the starting point of the message. 40 (buying stage), 43 (what the customer hates), 50 (wants, frustrations, willingness to pay) and 51 (read complaints) start from research on the audience.

### Covert influence and manipulation wording
Stations: [[06-priming-makes-people-buy-without-a-word]], [[12-emotions-are-caught-sell-the-feeling-first]], [[26-use-the-power-of-contrast]], [[35-make-them-feel-they-found-it]], [[36-confirmation-bias-say-what-people-already-think]], [[37-sell-the-problem-first]], [[29-sell-the-instant-benefit]], [[16-illusion-of-innovation]], [[38-framing-bundle-pricing-scarcity]], [[41-say-the-negative-first]], [[43-the-enemy-effect-builds-loyalty]], [[44-social-proof-reciprocity-branding]], [[48-the-so-you-can-method]], [[49-five-words-to-swap-in-marketing]]

Reason: All contain techniques or wording that work without the customer's awareness, or explicitly name manipulation; the main touchpoint with the deception checkpoint. 38 (framing, scarcity), 41 (order of facts), 43 ("trick" for loyalty), 44 (reciprocity, same product at five times the price), 48 ("feel dumb saying no", not usable) and 49 (word swaps that can restate a claim) all work through presentation.

### Content, attention and comment/DM calls to action
Stations: [[24-attention-comes-from-clarity-not-frequency]], [[25-emotional-content-is-remembered]], [[27-the-more-people-think-the-less-they-trust]], [[30-content-fails-in-the-first-seconds]], [[13-animations-that-turn-your-message-into-a-story]], [[10-the-five-year-old-rule]], [[05-study-what-works-particl]], [[39-people-scan-they-dont-read]], [[47-people-dont-read-they-scan]], [[40-four-buying-stages-schwartz]], [[48-the-so-you-can-method]]

Reason: All concern content on social channels and end with a comment or DM call to action (24, 25, 27, 30, 13, 10, 05). 39 (47 is its duplicate) and 40 concern content structure and stage; 48 ends with a comment call to action.

### Promotions and service offers
Stations: [[05-study-what-works-particl]], [[10-the-five-year-old-rule]], [[13-animations-that-turn-your-message-into-a-story]]

Reason: Each promotes a third-party tool (05) or the creator's own service (10, 13) with a DM call to action.

### Funnel diagnosis
Stations: [[32-conversion-problem-or-traffic-problem]], [[01-reduce-the-pain-of-paying]], [[14-sell-water-to-the-thirsty]], [[24-attention-comes-from-clarity-not-frequency]], [[51-you-dont-need-to-invent-to-become-rich]], [[40-four-buying-stages-schwartz]]

Reason: 32 splits problems into traffic and conversion; 01 (checkout) and 14, 24 (finding and getting attention) sit on either side. 51 ("people still need to discover and trust your business") is the traffic and conversion problem in one sentence; 40 matches content to the stage.

### Sell the outcome, not the product
Stations: [[02-sell-the-after-not-the-what]], [[33-people-buy-the-product-they-like-best]], [[11-you-sell-emotions-not-the-product]], [[42-sell-the-human-desire-behind-the-product]], [[45-sell-the-outcome-not-the-product]], [[48-the-so-you-can-method]], [[50-understand-why-money-moves]]

Reason: All say the buyer pays for the outcome or the desire behind the product. 45 is a near duplicate of 02 and 33, 42 of 11; 48 adds a formula (product + "so you can" + outcome), 50 the general principle (value is decided by the outcome).

### Named persuasion techniques with conditions
Stations: [[38-framing-bundle-pricing-scarcity]], [[41-say-the-negative-first]], [[44-social-proof-reciprocity-branding]], [[49-five-words-to-swap-in-marketing]], [[40-four-buying-stages-schwartz]], [[26-use-the-power-of-contrast]], [[36-confirmation-bias-say-what-people-already-think]]

Reason: All teach a named technique (framing, bundle pricing, scarcity, order of facts, social proof, reciprocity, word swaps, awareness stages). Each is usable only with true facts and full terms; 38 statement 8 (bundle with a false "free") and 44 statement 9 ("same thing" at five times the price) are not usable.

### Existing demand and where money moves
Stations: [[14-sell-water-to-the-thirsty]], [[40-four-buying-stages-schwartz]], [[50-understand-why-money-moves]], [[51-you-dont-need-to-invent-to-become-rich]], [[04-there-is-no-winning-product]], [[22-price-is-never-the-problem]]

Reason: All say money goes where a reason to buy already exists. 51 is a near duplicate of 14 in substance and adds "make what already sells slightly better"; 40 adds the buying stage; 50 states the principle.
