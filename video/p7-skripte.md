# P7 Short-video scripts (10 videos, German voice-over)

Status: draft for the owner (2026-10-28). Nothing published. Basis: GRUNDSATZ.md, IDEEN.md section 11, `quellen/nachfrage/web/p7-bewerbung.md` (called **p7** below), stations 08, 10, 13, 14, 24, 27, 29, 30, 34, 35, 37.

## Production rules for all 10 videos

**Format.** Vertical 9:16, 20-40 s. Voice-over (synthetic or the owner's voice, never shown), soft interface sounds (tick, click, whoosh) or silence. **No music** (rule 8).

**Visual style** (copied from `quellen/videos/10, 24, 30, 37/bild.md`): near-black background, thin white outline icons of objects with a soft glow, a single white dot as focus point, a new plain white sans-serif text line about every 3 s, one idea per frame. Allowed: circles, squares, lines, check mark, X, envelope, document, magnifier, calendar, lock, coin, clock, arrows, screen-style mockups (job ad and letter). **Not allowed:** people, faces, hands, avatars, figures, drawn or animated living beings (also no "person icon"; source video 37 uses one, we do not), no women, no photos. Do not copy the TikTok watermark or end card of the source creator.

**Hook rule (stations 30, 24, 27):** in the first 2 s text and voice say what the video is about, and the first frame is readable at once. Loops (station 08) are opened only on content (the answer comes within the video), never on price or terms.

**One video = one message = one action** (station 34): each video teaches one thing; the only action is the sign-up at the end.

**Standard end card (all videos, 5-6 s; the tool is NOT built):**
- On screen: "Wir bauen ein Tool: Stellenanzeige rein, passende Bewerbung raus." / "Noch nicht fertig. Vormerken ist kostenlos." / "Link im Profil" (once the page exists: the real address of the sign-up page on adrabic.com).
- Voice: "Wir bauen ein Tool, das aus Stellenanzeige und deinen Angaben ein passendes Anschreiben schreibt. Es ist noch nicht fertig. Wer es vormerken will: Link im Profil."
- Reasons: station 35 conditional (sales intent not hidden), stations 10/24 conditional (say what is offered and what happens with the contact: only e-mail for the launch notice, stated on the sign-up page), rule 1 (no claim that the product exists).
- Wording of the last sentence may vary; the three facts (planned, not finished, free sign-up) must stay.
- Forbidden: "bestes", "Nr. 1", "in 30 Sekunden fertig", "garantiert", "Tausende nutzen", numbers of sign-ups, deadlines, "nur noch X Plätze".

**Before the sign-up page goes live:** it needs the owner's Impressum and privacy notice (IDEEN 11). Price decided by the owner (2026-10-28): 9,90 € one-time for 30 days, no renewal. "Einmal zahlen, kein Abo" is a plan and is always worded as "geplant".

**Caption (all videos):** "Eigenes Projekt, noch nicht fertig. Beispieltexte sind ausgedacht." plus the sign-up link.

**Fact status.** p7 marks sources `(fetch)` or `[search summary, unverified]`. Rule here: unverified numbers are **not used**. Surveys are used only qualitatively. Competitor names are never shown or spoken.
