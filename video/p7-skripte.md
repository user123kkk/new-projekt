# P7 Short-video scripts (10 videos, German voice-over)

Status: draft for the owner (2026-10-28). Nothing published. Basis: GRUNDSATZ.md, IDEEN.md section 11, `quellen/nachfrage/web/p7-bewerbung.md` (called **p7** below), stations 08, 10, 13, 14, 24, 27, 29, 30, 34, 35, 37.

## Production rules for all 10 videos

**Format.** Vertical 9:16, 27-45 s (VIDEO-REGELN A1.3; actual lengths 28-30 s incl. end card). Voice-over (synthetic or the owner's voice, never shown) starts at 0.0 s, soft interface sounds (tick, click, whoosh) or silence. **No music** (rule 8). Target pace 2.5 German words/s (VIDEO-REGELN A2.2); to be re-checked on the first real voice-over. Beat length = spoken words / 2.5 s, at least 2.0 s, at most 3.0 s (so at most 7 words per beat). The voice level is normalised like the sources (A2.6).

**Visual style** (VIDEO-REGELN A4, A5, A8; copied from `quellen/videos/10, 24, 30, 37/bild.md`): near-black background, white and grey only, thin white outline icons of objects with a soft glow, **exactly one white dot per scene as focus point** (never over text), one idea per frame. Caption: one position and one font for all videos, at most 5 words (about 3), white sans-serif, changes with every beat. Every caption has a graphic of the same noun on screen at the same time; the graphic changes every beat (at most 3 s) by morph or fade, no flashes, no shakes. Allowed: circles, squares, lines, check mark, X, question mark, envelope, document, magnifier, calendar, lock, coin, clock, arrows, chart line, screen-style mockups (job ad and letter). **Not allowed:** people, faces, heads, hands, avatars, figures, eye, brain, smiley, cursor (VIDEO-REGELN O7 default), drawn or animated living beings, women, photos, colour, third-party logos. Do not copy the TikTok watermark or end card of the source creator.

**Hook rule (stations 30, 24, 27; B1):** voice and first caption start together at 0.0 s; the first spoken sentence names the topic and why it is worth watching; the first frame is readable at once. Loops (station 08) are opened only on content (video 5 "A oder B?", video 7 "drei Fragen") and are answered inside the video; every video ends on a complete sentence (C13). Never on price or terms.

**One video = one message = one action** (station 34): each video teaches one thing; the only action is the sign-up at the end.

**Standard end card (all videos, 2 beats, 5.2 s, 13 spoken words; the tool is NOT built):**
- Beat 1 (2.8 s): caption "Tool: noch nicht fertig", graphic mockup ad -> letter in a dashed (unfinished) frame, voice "Wir bauen ein Anschreiben-Tool. Noch nicht fertig."
- Beat 2 (2.4 s): caption "Vormerken kostenlos, Link im Profil", graphic envelope with check mark and link-chain icon, voice "Vormerken ist kostenlos. Link im Profil." (once the page exists: the real address of the sign-up page on adrabic.com).
- The three facts stay: planned tool, not finished, free sign-up with link in profile. The long wording ("Stellenanzeige rein, passende Bewerbung raus") is shortened to fit the pace; it is shown as the mockup graphic, not as text.
- Videos 7 and 8 insert the price beats between the two end beats (price, duration, renewal and cancellation always stated together, always "geplant"; GRUNDSATZ 3, B23).
- Reasons: station 35 conditional (sales intent not hidden), stations 10/24 conditional (say what is offered and what happens with the contact: only e-mail for the launch notice, stated on the sign-up page), rule 1 (no claim that the product exists).
- Forbidden: "bestes", "Nr. 1", "in 30 Sekunden fertig", "garantiert", "Tausende nutzen", numbers of sign-ups, deadlines, "nur noch X Plätze".

**Before the sign-up page goes live:** it needs the owner's Impressum and privacy notice (IDEEN 11). Price decided by the owner (2026-10-28): 9,90 € one-time for 30 days, no renewal. "Einmal zahlen, kein Abo" is a plan and is always worded as "geplant".

**Caption (all videos):** "Eigenes Projekt, noch nicht fertig. Beispieltexte sind ausgedacht." plus the sign-up link.

**Fact status.** p7 marks sources `(fetch)` or `[search summary, unverified]`. Rule here: unverified numbers are **not used**. Surveys are used only qualitatively. Competitor names are never shown or spoken.
