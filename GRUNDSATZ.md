# Foundation

Status: draft. The project owner sets the final version and the exceptions.

## Order of authority
1. Quran and Sunnah as understood by the Salaf as-Salih.
2. Then the sources in `quellen/`. Every statement there is measured against point 1.
3. Then our own reasoning.

A marketing technique is not adopted because it works. It is adopted if it is permissible and it works.

## Assessment of every statement from the sources
Each sentence of a station is rated on its own:
- **usable**: permissible, can be used as is.
- **conditional**: the core is usable, the execution must change. The change is noted.
- **not usable**: the reason is noted.

Checkpoints (open list, will be extended):
- Deception (ghish): "Whoever deceives us is not one of us" (Muslim). No false claims about scarcity, deadlines, prices, reviews or user numbers.
- Riba: no interest-based financing or installment models.
- Gharar: no contracts with an unclear subject or hidden terms. Subscriptions and trials must be clear.
- Najsh: no artificial price anchors or fake offers to push buyers.
- Content: no music, no impermissible imagery, no promotion of the forbidden.
- Religious obligations are not treated as a market gap.
