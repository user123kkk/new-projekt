# Foundation

Status: fixed (2026-10-27). Changes only by the project owner.

## Order of authority
1. Quran and Sunnah as understood by the Salaf as-Salih. The scholars who learned and taught by this methodology fall under this point.
2. Then the sources in `quellen/`. Every statement there is measured against point 1.
3. Then our own reasoning.

A marketing technique is not adopted because it works. It is adopted if it is permissible and it works.

## Assessment of every statement from the sources
Each sentence of a station is rated on its own:
- **usable**: permissible, can be used as is.
- **conditional**: the core is usable, the execution must change. The change is noted.
- **not usable**: the reason is noted.

## Rules

### Honesty and contracts
1. **Deception (ghish):** "Whoever deceives us is not one of us" (Muslim). No false claims about scarcity, deadlines, prices, reviews or user numbers.
2. **Riba:** no interest-based financing or installment models, no buy-now-pay-later offers.
3. **Gharar:** subscriptions and trials are clear before purchase: duration, price afterwards, how to cancel. No hidden terms.
4. **Najsh:** no artificial price anchors, no invented "was" prices, no fake offers to push buyers.
5. **Exaggeration:** claims like "best", "number 1" or "revolutionary" only with proof. Otherwise not allowed.
6. **Privacy:** no hidden tracking, no dark patterns. Anything that would have to be concealed from the user falls under ghish.

### Psychology
7. Psychological mechanisms from the sources (priming, contrast, halo effect, loss aversion, open loops, etc.) are allowed when the statement is true and nothing is concealed. They are not allowed when the aim is to move a person against their own interest (see station 36: "manipulate").

### Content
8. **Music:** none in any own content, neither marketing nor app. Voice, sounds and silence are allowed.
9. **Images of living beings:** default is none. Use shapes, text, icons and abstract graphics. Photos or video of living beings only where there is a real need. No drawings or animations of living beings. This includes faceless pictogram figures (stick figures, circle-head icons) — owner decision 2026-10-28. A character behind the brand exists only as voice and personality, never as a drawn or animated figure.
10. **Women:** no depiction in marketing or the app.
11. **Religious obligations** are not treated as a market gap.

### Channels and money
12. **Platforms:** posting permissible content and running ads on platforms such as TikTok or Instagram is allowed. The wrong done by others on the platform is not attributed to the one who posts what is right.
13. **Ads inside the own app:** not allowed. Third-party ads cannot be controlled. Revenue comes only from sales or subscriptions.
14. **Payments:** accepting card and app store payments (Stripe, Apple, Google) is allowed. No financing offers to customers.
15. **Target group:** selling to everyone, Muslim or not, is allowed as long as the product is halal.

## Exceptions
None at this time.
